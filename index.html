Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-18

About

Bike Trails is an offline cycle game developed by SDX STUDIO ("we", "us", "our"). This short privacy policy explains how we handle data in the Bike Trails game.

No Personal Data Collection

Bike Trails is designed to run offline and does not collect, transmit, or store personal data to our servers. The game does not require an account, login, or network connection for normal play.

Local Data Storage

The game may store non-personal data locally on your device for the purpose of gameplay, such as game progress, settings, and high scores. This data remains on your device unless you choose to delete the app or game files.

Analytics and Third Parties

We do not use analytics services, advertising networks, or other third-party trackers in the offline release of Bike Trails.

Children

Bike Trails is suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child’s personal information has been collected, please contact us (see below).

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. If changes affect how personal data is handled, we will update the effective date and post the revised policy here.
